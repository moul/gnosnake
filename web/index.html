You do not submit a score. You submit the moves.

reading only — no wallet needed

score 0 ticks 0 press an arrow key to start

How this works

Take a seed from the realm, play, then send the moves. The realm replays them and computes the score itself, so nobody can claim a score their moves did not produce and anybody can re-derive every line of the board.

What that does not prove is that a human played. A bot's moves replay exactly as well as a person's. The realm says so on its own page rather than implying otherwise.
